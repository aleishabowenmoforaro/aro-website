Rhythm

Understand your natural rhythm

Rhythm in Aro centres around awareness of your menstrual cycle and how it influences mood, energy, focus, and emotional wellbeing throughout the month.

By tracking patterns over time, you can begin to recognise recurring shifts and respond with greater self-understanding and compassion.

What you can track

Cycle phases

Log cycle phases and key dates to build awareness of how your body and mind change across the month.

Mood & emotions

Notice emotional patterns and fluctuations, helping you reflect on how hormones may influence how you feel day to day.

Energy & wellbeing

Track energy, rest, and overall wellbeing to better understand when you feel more expansive, reflective, or in need of support.

Rather than predicting or prescribing, Aro supports gentle reflection, helping you build a clearer picture of your rhythm and make choices that feel more aligned over time.
