Movement

Train with your cycle, not against it

Movement in Aro is built around one simple idea: your training shouldn’t feel the same every day of the month — because your body doesn’t.

What you can track

Log runs, strength sessions, Pilates, yoga, HIIT, and recovery — while Aro helps you spot patterns between performance, energy levels, and your cycle.

Strength & workouts

Track gym sessions, sets, reps, and training intensity across the month.

Running & cardio

Log distance, pace, effort, and how movement feels in different phases.

Recovery & rest

Balance performance with rest days, mobility, and lower-impact movement.

Movement that adapts with you

Instead of forcing consistency through burnout, Aro helps you work with natural shifts in strength, motivation, and recovery — so training feels supportive, not punishing.
