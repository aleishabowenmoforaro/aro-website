Movement

Train with your cycle, not against it

Movement in Aro. is built around one simple idea: your body is not the same every day of the month, so your training shouldn’t be either.

Instead of forcing consistency through burnout, Aro. helps you understand how strength, endurance, motivation, and recovery naturally fluctuate across your cycle.

What you can track

Log runs, strength sessions, Pilates, yoga, HIIT, and recovery. See patterns emerge between performance, energy levels, and cycle phases... without judgement.

Why it works

When movement aligns with your physiology, progress feels more sustainable. You train harder when your body supports it, and recover properly when it doesn’t.
