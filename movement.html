Movement

Train with your cycle, not against it

Movement in Aro is built around one simple idea, your body changes throughout the month, and your movement experience changes with it.

Rather than following rigid plans, Aro helps you notice how strength, endurance, motivation, and recovery naturally shift across your cycle, supporting a more intuitive relationship with movement.

What you can track

Training & workouts

Log runs, strength sessions, Pilates, yoga, and HIIT in a way that reflects how your body feels.

Energy & performance

Notice how performance and motivation shift across different days and phases of your cycle.

Recovery & rest

Track rest, lighter movement, and recovery without pressure, guilt, or rigid targets.

Instead of instructions, Aro offers subtle cues and reflections, helping you decide when to push, when to slow down, and when rest might be what your body needs most.
