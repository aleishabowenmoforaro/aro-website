Founder’s story

Why Aro exists

Aro was created from a growing awareness that many people are expected to move, work, and live as if their bodies are static — when in reality, they are anything but.

Over time, it became clear that there was a gap between how people are encouraged to care for their health and how their bodies actually function, particularly when it comes to hormonal and cyclical changes.

Aro is not about fixing bodies or optimising productivity. It is about creating space for understanding, reflection, and more compassionate choices — both personally and, eventually, within wider systems.

Looking ahead

What began as a personal project is growing into something broader, shaped by conversations, research, and lived experience.

As Aro develops, the intention is to support individuals, founders, and organisations in engaging with cycle awareness in ways that are ethical, respectful, and genuinely useful — without reducing people to data points or expectations.

This is just the beginning.
