Fuel

Fuel in tune with your body

Fuel in Aro is about building awareness of how your body responds to food across different phases of your cycle, not following strict rules or perfect plans.

By noticing patterns in appetite, energy, digestion, and cravings, you can begin to make more informed, supportive choices that feel aligned with your body.

What you can track

Meals & nourishment

Log meals and snacks in a flexible way, focusing on how food supports your energy, satisfaction, and wellbeing.

Hunger & energy cues

Notice how hunger, fullness, and energy levels shift throughout your cycle and across different days.

Patterns & reflections

Reflect on how different foods make you feel over time, helping you recognise patterns without judgment or pressure.

Rather than telling you what to eat, Aro supports you in tuning into your body’s signals, encouraging curiosity, consistency, and trust in your own experience.
